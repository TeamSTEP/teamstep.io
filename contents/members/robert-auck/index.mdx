## Who am I?

I am the lead developer of Team STEP. All developers will come to me and ask for advice.

## How to work with me

1. Beg, borrow, steal, buy, fabricate or otherwise obtain sacrifice for the almighty Robert.
2. Place me on our desk and inform me you are just going to go over some code with it, if that’s all right.
3. Explain to me what your code is supposed to do, and then go into detail and explain your code line by line.
4. At some point you will tell me what you are doing next and then realise that that is not in fact what you are actually doing. I will sit there serenely, happy in the knowledge that I helped you on your way.
