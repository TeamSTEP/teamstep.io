About

We are a team of aspiring indie game developers from all around the world. We love to create games that combine art and technology. We believe that fun people make enjoyable games, so our goal is to become an indie studio that can allow people with creative minds to work freely and help other aspiring artists.

Esconder

Esconder is a 2D top-down stealth-puzzle shooter game made with Game Maker Studio. We developed this project to compete in the National Olympiad Informatics in the project category.

View

Slack In Your Time

Slack In Your Time is a Slack Application that automatically detects messages that references time and converts them to the full date of all the channel member's timezone.

View

Repeat

A story-driven point-and-click adventure/visual novel made with Ren'py. It tells a story about a young boy reading into the past of his grandfather to find the answer that his passed away grandmother left for her adoring husband. But he uncovers the old man's regretful past and a conspiracy that is much greater than what it seemed like.

View

Palette

Palette is a 2D side-scroller platformer made in RPG Maker VX Ace. This game tries the impossible by creating a 2D side-scroller with a full physics engine for a game engine that was primarily meant for creating a simple 2D top-down RPG without coding. But we did it with Ruby.

View

Witch One

Witch One is a online stealth-focused battle-royal inspired strategy game where you try to catch people from the back without getting noticed
